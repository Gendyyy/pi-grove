What moves on a running row

Four directions for the mark that says “still working”. All four keep one cell of width, repaint at 100 ms, paint in the theme’s warning role, and stop the instant the call or phase ends. Only the motion changes.

Breath. The dot never changes shape; its brightness rides a cosine between the warning colour and the muted colour, 1.2 s a cycle, twelve samples. Each row breathes on its own clock, so two live calls look staggered.

Axis: what moves on a running mark — motion curve and clock, not palette or layout. Content is identical in all four tabs.

Assumptions: audience is the grove maintainer choosing what ships; register is terminal instrument, so the artifact wears pi’s own dark and light role colours (amber warning, violet accent, green success, muted/dim secondaries) rendered in the browser; the hard constraint is one cell of width, a fixed 100 ms repaint, both terminal appearances, and the classic card as the only layout.

Out of scope: the removal of the shimmer sweep, the deletion of the four extra layouts, and the 5-minute freeze — all decided already, none drawn here.
