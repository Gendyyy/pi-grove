What all four keep

The task title stays in the frame or on the headline line and never rewrites per call; progress is derived from the phase plan, never stored; a failed call keeps its own marker and the card only turns red when everything failed; the meter stays the click target for the plan and the rail for the rows. The newest call is never hidden, and its description rides the closed card.

Palette is pi's dark theme by role — accent for live work and the meter, syntaxVariable for read/grep, syntaxFunction for edit, syntaxKeyword for bash, success/error/warning for outcomes only. No direction introduces a colour that isn't already a theme token.
